What's New

v0.38.5 Contact Us

Capture without interruption, organize later with flexible tags and filters, connect ideas to songs, and rediscover forgotten sparks.

New

• Added a Contact us row in Settings
• Added the Meadow Works Studio support email
• Tapping the address opens the device’s default email app
• Updated the About section with Mosaic’s current tagline

Contact

Questions, ideas, and bug reports can now be sent to meadowworksstudio@gmail.com directly from Settings.

Notes

The mail link uses the device’s configured email application. Mosaic does not send messages or collect contact information itself.

Known limitations

Libraries remain stored on this device until account sync is introduced. Export backups regularly.
